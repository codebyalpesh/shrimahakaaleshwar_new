Open index.html in your browser, or upload the website folder contents to a static web host.

Updated features:
- Appointment requests through WhatsApp only. Review the details, continue to WhatsApp, then press Send there.
- Sticky main navigation while scrolling.
- Top contact bar with mobile number, email address and daily hours, each with a small 12px icon.
- Smooth page-load and one-time viewport animations, including forms, cards and footer groups.
- Reduced-motion support and keyboard-friendly reveals.
- English, Hindi and Marathi language selection.

Contact email links open your email application only; appointment details are not emailed. No email credentials or server are required.
Appointments require manual confirmation. No payment or booking database is included.
